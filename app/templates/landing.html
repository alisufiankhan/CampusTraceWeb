Integrated Campus Integrity Workflow

Providing a secure loop from reporting an item to verification and safe return.

1. Report Found Valuables

Did you discover a lost phone, wallet, or ID card around the PAF-IAST library, cafeteria, or labs? Instantly log details into our student reports queue for admin review.

2. Admin Verification

Our university administrators verify incoming student reports against physical custody. Once verified, the items become searchable on the main student portal.

3. Claim & Handover

Search and claim your lost item. Provide detailed proof of ownership (e.g. locks patterns, serial keys, physical marks). Administrators review and process physically witnessed handovers.

Our Campus Integrity Standard

Honesty and integrity are core pillars of Pak-Austria Fachhochschule. CampusTrace ensures all students behave responsibly. Multiple false or speculative claims trigger automatic flagging restrictions (3 rejections block claim submissions), keeping the portal reliable and trusted.
