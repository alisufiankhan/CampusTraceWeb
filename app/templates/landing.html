How it Works

Get your belongings back in three simple, secure steps.

1. Report

Log in and post details about the item you lost or found on campus.

2. Find

Search our campus database or wait for a verified match notification.

3. Get Back

Coordinate a verified, safe handover at the admin office securely.

Built for Peace of Mind

Everything you need to safely and securely manage lost and found items on campus.

Formal Claims

Submit official claim requests with supporting descriptions before contacting finders.

Verified Listings

All items must be posted by verified PAFI-IAST student or staff accounts.

Dispute Resolution

Admin mediation for conflicting claims on high-value items like electronics.

Auto Expiry

Unclaimed items are automatically archived after 30 days to keep the feed clean.

Accountability

Track exactly who reported, claimed, and authorized the return of every item.

Handover Proof

Digital signatures required upon item collection for an unbreakable audit trail.

Designed for the Whole Campus

For Students

  • Quickly search for your lost items across campus.
  • Get notified instantly if someone finds something matching your description.
  • Safe handover process—no meeting strangers in random places.
  • Do the right thing easily when you find someone's belongings.

For Admin & Staff

  • Centralized dashboard to manage all lost property.
  • Reduce front desk clutter and disorganized item storage.
  • Maintain digital logs and signatures for compliance and liability.
  • Automated reports on campus lost-and-found statistics.
